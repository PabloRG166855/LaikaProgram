# Mission Design Lab (NASA Goddard)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Title** | Mission Design Lab (MDL) |
| **Author / Org** | NASA Goddard Space Flight Center, Engineering and Technology Directorate (ETD), Integrated Design Center (IDC) |
| **Date** | Page last updated July 23, 2026 |
| **Link** | https://etd.gsfc.nasa.gov/capabilities/integrated-design-center/mission-design-lab/ |

**Purpose:** "NASA's Mission Design Lab (MDL) provides a collaborative engineering environment for the development of space flight designs and architecture concepts." It brings together people, process, tools and a facility to quickly produce design products tailored to the customer.

> Note: the page is short. It names no software tools, costs, study counts or direct contacts.

### 1. Study options (from lightest to most detailed)

| Study type | Time | Products | Entry / scope |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Office Hours** | ~1 hour | Brainstorming; identify "tall poles" (biggest problems) and key trade studies; recommend next steps and an engineering point of contact | Little or no prep |
| **Concept Development** | A few half days | Concept feasibility, risk assessment | Scope set by Concept Maturity Level (CML) |
| **Tailored Trade Study** | Variable | Concept, ConOps, resources summary, trade tree, possibly a ROM (rough order of magnitude) cost | Scope set by CML |
| **Point Design Study** | 6 days | CAD, Master Equipment List (MEL), engineering models, CEMA cost | Must "pass" the CML checklist |

### 2. Link to Concept Maturity Levels

- CMLs were "initially defined by NASA's Jet Propulsion Laboratory (JPL)." The page defines a CML as "a measure of how developed, tested, and ready a concept, idea, or technology is for implementation or deployment."
- "IDC studies can be tailored to support concept maturation from **CML 1 through CML 4**."
- The page links to the JPL CML dataset: https://dataverse.jpl.nasa.gov/dataset.xhtml?persistentId=hdl:2014/44299. Summary of that paper: [concept-maturity-levels.md](concept-maturity-levels.md).

### 3. What the MDL can design (mission scope)

- **Orbits:** LEO, GEO, libration points, retrograde, drift-away, lunar, deep space
- **Architectures:** a single spacecraft, constellations, formation flying, distributed systems
- **Ground system:** concept development, services and products
- **Launch vehicle:** accommodations (fitting the spacecraft on the launch vehicle)
- **End of mission:** controlled or uncontrolled de-orbit, reconnaissance and landing, sample return

### 4. Discipline engineering team (16 disciplines)

| | | | |
|---|---|---|---|
| Attitude Control | Mission Cost | Propulsion | Avionics |
| Mechanical Designer | Reliability | Flight Dynamics | Flight Software |
| Mechanical Systems | Orbital Debris | Radiation Environment | Structural |
| Communications | Mission Operations | Power Systems | Thermal |

**What each subsystem delivers:** requirements; baseline design; alternative designs and trade studies; functional diagrams; interfaces; detailed **mass, power and data-rate estimates**; technical risk assessment; issues and concerns; recommendations; models; parametric or grassroots costs; background.

### 5. Typical outputs

Spacecraft system block diagrams · Orbit analysis · Communication and ground system analysis · Launch system accommodations · Spacecraft Master Equipment List (MEL) · Mechanical packaging / CAD model · 3D observatory concept models
